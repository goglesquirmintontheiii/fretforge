Fretforge 0.6.0 bundles two phases of the plan: 0.5.0 (AI tabs from recordings) and 0.6.0 (the rig editor), together with the requests made for 0.2.0. Later phases move up: the next release is 0.7.0.

## Highlights

- **AI tabs.** A recording or a YouTube link becomes a multi-track tab: guitar, bass, drums, piano on two staves, vocal melody with lyrics, and other instruments. Bars follow the music's own tempo, strings and frets come from a fingering model that keeps shapes playable, and bends, vibrato, slides, hammer-ons, palm mutes and drum ghost notes are marked. The recording and its stems come attached and synced. Set tuning, capo, time signature, pickup, BPM and feel, or leave them automatic.
- **Review AI tabs.** Uncertain notes are marked, A/B switches between the tab and the instrument's stem on a loop, bars can be accepted one by one, and the tab stays private until you share it. Your corrections teach the fingering model.
- **Easier versions** of any tab as extra revisions: power-chord rhythm, root-note bass, simple drums, slower tempo.
- **AI track autoswitch.** A track with an AI stem can play the stem instead of the synth: always, never, or while it's soloed.
- **Rig editor.** Build as many rigs as you like from about 70 blocks (drives, dynamics, EQ, modulation, delay, reverb, pitch, filters, wah) plus NAM amps, preamps, pedals and cabs and IRs, on two paths with parallel lanes. Snapshots, setlists, MIDI learn, expression, looper, tuner, global EQ, Eco/Standard/High quality, Helix `.hlx` import.
- **Rigs on tab tracks.** Every instrument can use its own rig, and switch rigs or snapshots at any bar.
- **NAM models by role** (amp, preamp, cab, pedal, outboard), `.zip` packs, and **model search** by tone, genre and type across TONE3000 and community collections. The desktop app embeds TONE3000 with an ＋ Fretforge button next to every download.
- **Choose your audio input and output.** The desktop live rig is a JACK client named `Fretforge`, so Helvum or qpwgraph can route it.
- **YouTube in the player.** Songsterr's synced videos come with Songsterr imports, and any YouTube link can be added. The video floats over the score and follows play, pause, seeking, loops and speed.
- **Search** shows one merged list with a badge per site and every version of a song. New: GuitarTabs.cc, and links into Guitar Pro mySongBook and guitar.ch.
- **Library filters** by strings (6–9 and 12-string guitar, 4–6-string bass) with all common tunings, guitar type and tone.
- **A floating header** with a slide-out menu and a floating search (Ctrl K) on every page, including the player.

## Fixes

- Ultimate Guitar's browser check no longer fails over and over: the desktop app shows the site and you complete the check yourself, then searches work again.
- Moving a track's volume slider doesn't interrupt playback any more (alphaTab re-seeked on every change).
- The tab/notation/both view is now one setting for every song, and the current-bar highlight is subtler (Settings → Player).

## Known limits

- AI tabs are a starting point. With the optional models (Basic Pitch, Beat This!, faster-whisper, installed by Settings → AI tools) results are better; without them Fretforge uses its built-in methods, which handle clear parts but struggle with dense mixes. Harmonics aren't detected yet. The transcription and fingering were tested on synthetic recordings; real Demucs runs and YouTube downloads couldn't run in the build environment.
- TONE3000 search uses only what its free API tier allows: trending and latest lists, your favorites, downloads and uploads, and its tone picker. Full-text search across TONE3000 needs its own site (embedded in the desktop app).
- Guitar Pro mySongBook and guitar.ch are paid catalogs: Fretforge links to them but can't open their tabs.
- YouTube videos need an internet connection and play in YouTube's embedded player; some videos don't allow embedding. Pitch shift and the metronome don't apply to videos; speed is limited to 0.25x–2x.
- Live sites (Songsterr videos, GuitarTabs.cc, the catalogs, the browser-check window), JACK routing with Helvum and real audio interfaces were tested against stand-ins, not live.
