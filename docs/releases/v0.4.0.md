Fretforge 0.4.0 bundles three phases of the plan: 0.3.0 (accounts, sharing, editor), 0.4.0 (recordings and AI stems) and what was planned as 0.6.0 (NAM amps, cabinet IRs and a low-latency engine). Later phases move up one number.

## Download

```sh
chmod +x Fretforge-0.4.0-x86_64.AppImage && ./Fretforge-0.4.0-x86_64.AppImage
```

Docker: `ghcr.io/goglesquirmintontheiii/fretforge:v0.4.0` (amd64 and arm64).

## Highlights

- **NAM amps on tab tracks.** Press AMP on a guitar or bass track, pick a `.nam` model and a cabinet IR, and the track plays through them. Tracks are rendered once and processed in the background on all cores, so playback stays light, and seeking, loops, slow-down and pitch shift stay instant.
- **Rig page.** A shared library of NAM models and IRs with tags and presets; preview on a built-in riff; play live through the desktop app's native JACK engine (PipeWire on Nobara) or in the browser; loopback latency test; train captures from a reamp.
- **Recordings and AI stems.** Attach a recording or a YouTube link, split it into guitar, bass, drums, piano, vocals and other with Demucs (plus a lead/rhythm guitar split), mix the stems, and sync the recording to the tab automatically or by tapping along.
- **Accounts.** Owner, member and viewer roles, invite links and OIDC single sign-on; favorites, practice settings, history and playlists are personal.
- **Tab editor** with revisions you can compare and switch between, comments with @mentions, share links, exports (Guitar Pro 7, MIDI, alphaTex, PDF via print) and offline use as an installable web app.

## Upgrading

The library database moves to a new format on first start. On a server, the existing password becomes the owner account "Owner"; sign in with that name (or leave the name empty while you're the only account) and rename yourself in Settings. Favorites, practice settings and history move to that account.

## Engine

forge-dsp is Fretforge's own C++ audio engine, compiled to WASM (SIMD) for the browser and natively for the desktop. Its NAM runtime reads WaveNet A1 and A2 features, LSTM, Linear, ConvNet and slimmable containers and matches NeuralAmpModelerCore to about 1e-6 on its example models. IR convolution has no added latency.

## Known limits

- Stem separation, auto-sync, YouTube audio and NAM training need the AI tools (Settings → AI tools installs them, about 2–3 GB with PyTorch). They were tested with a stand-in worker and the DSP parts directly; the full Demucs download and a real YouTube fetch could not be exercised in the build environment.
- The live JACK engine was tested against a JACK server without audio hardware (including the loopback latency measurement); real-interface latency depends on your PipeWire/JACK buffer.
- OIDC was tested against a minimal mock provider.
- In recording mode the tab's synth tracks don't play along yet; switch between "Tab sound" and "Recording".
- Drum tracks can't be edited in the editor yet, and new tabs start with a guitar or bass track. MusicXML export and the UI translations (i18n) are not in this release.
- Rig quality "Eco" uses the lighter variant of models that ship several (slimmable A2 models). Oversampling modes wait for the built-in drive blocks (now 0.6.0).
