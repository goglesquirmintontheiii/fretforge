First release: the tab player and library, with Songsterr and Ultimate Guitar search built in.

### Install on Nobara / Fedora
```sh
chmod +x Fretforge-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage
./Fretforge-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage
```
It needs `fuse3` (installed by default on Nobara/Fedora); `libfuse2` is not required. It runs natively on Wayland; use `FRETFORGE_X11=1` to force XWayland.

Your library folder is `~/Music/Fretforge`: drop Guitar Pro, MusicXML or MIDI files in and they appear within seconds.

### What's in it
- **Library:** watched folder, drag-and-drop import, typo-tolerant search, instrument filter, tuning badges, favorites, recently played.
- **One search box** for your library, Songsterr (full multi-track tabs that play) and Ultimate Guitar (free chord sheets and text tabs). Opening a result saves it to your library for offline use.
- **Player:**
  - speed 15–200% and speed trainer
  - bar-snapped loops
  - per-track volume, mute and solo, plus master volume
  - transpose (rewrites frets and chords) and pitch shift (sound only)
  - count-in, metronome, tuner
  - tab, notation or both views
  - fretboard (left-handed option), chords panel with capo, lyrics
  - follow cursor, fullscreen, section jump, keyboard shortcuts
- **Chord sheets:** transpose, capo, chord diagrams, auto-scroll.
- **Docker image** for running it as a server: `ghcr.io/goglesquirmintontheiii/fretforge`.

### Known limits
- TuxGuitar (.tg) and Power Tab (.ptb) files need converting to GP5 first.
- Ultimate Guitar Pro, Official and Guitar Pro tabs need a paid UG account and aren't fetched.
- Count-in is one bar (alphaTab's limit).
- Songsterr and UG can change their sites at any time; a broken source shows an error and the rest keeps working.
